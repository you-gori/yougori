YOUGORI SWARM MINING LEGAL MEMORANDUM

To: Yougori project maintainer
From: Codex implementation and legal research draft for qualified counsel
Date: 10 October 2026
Re: Direct external payments and authorized offline source research
Rules version: 2026-10-10.3

Recommendation: operate Swarm as an authorized offline source-review service,
with publishers making and paying their own external USDC commitments. Remove
Yougori from receipt, custody, balances, signing and transfer control for new
bounty rewards. Preserve historic obligations rather than relabeling them.
Obtain exact publisher authorization and participant consent before delivering
source or running work. This changes the activity that creates exposure; it
does not merely rename a financial service or insert a blanket disclaimer.

This is a researched risk-reduction design and implementation review, not a
signed lawyer's opinion, privileged communication or worldwide legal clearance.
The maintainer requested text. The Background, Analysis and Conclusion structure
follows the supplied Legal Memorandum template. Classification follows the
actual operation and applicable law, not a claim that the service is open source.

BACKGROUND

1. Operator and scope

The maintainer says Yougori is individually built, has no registered legal
company name, and is intended for worldwide users. No operator legal name,
service address, governing law, insurance or regulatory authorization has been
supplied. Nothing here invents a company, a country clause or a registration.
The public client code is AGPL-3.0-only; the hosted coordination backend is
private. The user authorized actual changes to reduce exposure for payments
and bug hunting. All changes remain local; no push or deployment was requested.

Swarm's new direct-payment flow is separate from Neo Grid's paid inference
system. That other system still has its own deposits, earnings, withdrawals,
historic liabilities and regulatory questions. This redesign does not exempt it.

2. New reward operation

The named publisher records a USDC promise and acknowledges that Yougori has
not verified or reserved its funds. It offers compensation for a qualifying
report under the exact accepted criteria, not a token sale or investment return.
After review and appeals, an award records the publisher's external payment
obligation. It is not a Yougori account balance or earnings credit.
New publication and scope versions require nonempty written payment terms.
Missing terms cannot change a prior accepted document or revoke its membership;
the server inserts no hidden payment deadline or promise of automatic payment.

The publisher pays the winner from its own wallet outside Yougori. New Swarm
programs provide no deposit collection, reward escrow, private-key access,
signing, transfer initiation/control, withdrawal or reward-linked commission.
Swarm does not construct a payment in a Yougori wallet transaction flow.

Publisher transaction evidence is publisher_reported_unverified. The winner
separately acknowledges receipt: winner_acknowledged_receipt records that
statement. chainVerified remains false. None of those records is a platform
guarantee of funds, legal compliance, receipt on a chain or eventual payment.

3. Historic obligations

Earlier reserves, credited awards, receipts and obligations remain recorded.
They are not silently converted into unfunded promises, erased, forfeited, sent
or refunded. New work on those programs is blocked pending manual resolution;
existing reports and review/appeal history remain available for disputes.
An explicit resolution request records a pending case and outstanding amount.
It is not a statement that money has been returned. Existing actual funds still
need operator reconciliation. Redesign cannot remove liabilities from past conduct.

4. Source authorization and access

New programs require the actual publisher identity, signer and capacity, rights
statement, defensive purpose and permitted offline tests. Authorization is
bound to the exact source revision, digest and terms version, with its own
digest. Server-owned minimum exclusions prohibit live systems, third-party
targets and production secrets. The declaration is publisher-declared, not
platform verification of identity, source ownership or legal authority.

Source requires publisher-approved participants. New scope/source versions
require fresh approval and acceptance. Participants expressly accept source
authorization, exact rules and direct-payment risk. API callers cannot obtain
source or start work by omitting frontend checkboxes. A metadata-only application
does not grant source or execution. Scripted CLI acceptance requires separate
authorization and direct-payment flags as well as the terms and rules digest.

Work remains local source review and bounded declared offline checks in a
separate test sandbox. Live production, host/LAN/cloud targets and unrelated
participants are excluded. No arbitrary shell or external target scanning is
granted. Known secret file paths/private-key content are refused. Inspection
is not proof that every sensitive datum has been removed. Model instructions,
project text and human suggestions cannot enlarge scope or defeat stop controls.

5. Agreements and disclosures

Rules version 2026-10-10.3 preserves software-license modification rights and
prohibits malicious use, safeguard bypasses, false attribution and falsified
records in the hosted service. The publisher grants limited research permission
and, to the extent lawful, promises not to assert its own civil claims solely
for strictly compliant research. This cannot bind third parties or authorities,
waive mandatory duties or authorize harm outside scope. Ownership is retained;
no blanket private-source redistribution or training permission is imposed.

Production Swarm requires operator legal identity, type, service contact and
address, feature enablement and an independent appeal-review account. The
operator may be an actual individual or entity. Configuration does not register
a company, verify identity or grant legal approval. Missing disclosures keep
production Swarm disabled. Development fixtures are not a real operator.

ANALYSIS

6. Payment exposure is reduced by avoiding the actual activity

MiCA defines custody through safekeeping/control of assets or access on behalf
of clients, and transfer services through transfers between addresses/accounts
on behalf of others. Inference: removing those activities from Swarm removes
important factual elements of the prior design's custody/transfer concern.
Other services, actual control and applicable jurisdictions still need assessment.
Authority, Article 3:
https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mica/article-3-definitions

FinCEN distinguishes software production/distribution itself from a business
accepting and transmitting value, with classification based on facts and
circumstances. Inference: recording an externally paid research reward without
fund/key control materially differs from the former treasury, balance and
withdrawal service. This is not a conclusive US or state-law exemption.
Authority, FIN-2014-R002:
https://www.fincen.gov/resources/statutes-regulations/administrative-rulings/application-fincens-regulations-virtual

Do not add a Yougori-controlled release key, upgradeable escrow, publisher-funded
wallet, custody account, hosted signing, transaction routing or automatic reward
settlement and keep claiming this boundary. A noncustodial label does not decide
whether the actual service controls another person's transfer. A research-review
decision should not become authority to move money.

The design does not rely on a fully decentralized exception. ESMA describes
that exception as requiring no intermediary, assessed case by case. Yougori
operates central coordination. Open source and wallet-to-wallet payments do
not by themselves make the hosted service fully decentralized. Authority:
https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-data/questions-answers/2671

7. Counterparty obligations remain real

A publisher can fail to pay, dispute a decision, make a wallet mistake or submit
false evidence. Participants expressly accept this risk before work. Do not
market new rewards as funded, escrowed, insured or guaranteed. Do not turn a
transaction hash into a platform-verified payment claim.

Accepted terms should identify the actual publisher, amount, USDC network,
qualifying criteria, duplicate rules, receipt priority, deadlines, payment due
date and review/appeal contact. Preserve older report rights when scope changes.
Service restrictions and insolvency do not automatically erase valid obligations.
Internal appeals are not a waiver of non-waivable external remedies.

Payers and recipients have their own tax, wallet, sanctions and payment duties.
Yougori must still assess duties from its own conduct and nexus. Naming the
publisher as debtor matches the new operation; it does not remove liability
for Yougori's own wrongdoing or misleading representations. This review does
not determine that every USDC transaction is permissible everywhere.

8. Authorization reduces research exposure

The EU information-systems framework distinguishes conduct lacking relevant
authorization/legal right. Publishers cannot give rights they do not possess.
Inference: an exact lawfully supplied local snapshot with written permission
avoids many live-target access issues, while source rights and other duties
remain. Authority, Directive 2013/40/EU:
https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2013/40/oj/eng

The DOJ vulnerability-disclosure framework recommends defining scope, handling
sensitive data and examining third-party interests. It creates no enforceable
rights and focuses on online systems; it is design guidance, not clearance for
this local-source pilot. Authority:
https://www.justice.gov/media/905211/dl?inline=

The DOJ's own VDP illustrates a scoped non-assertion commitment while preserving
independent third-party and prosecutorial authority. It does not cover Yougori.
Only the named publisher promises limited non-assertion here, within its own
rights and the exact permitted lawful research. Authority:
https://www.justice.gov/jmd/vulnerability-disclosure-policy

The DOJ's CFAA good-faith research policy is not immunity for bad-faith conduct
such as extortion or a resolution of other countries' laws/private claims.
Authority:
https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/department-justice-announces-new-policy-charging-cases-under-computer-fraud-and-abuse-act

Maintain the technical local-only boundary, declared checks, budget/stop controls,
honest evidence and incident handling. An unrestricted model or a publisher's
checkbox is not permission to attack others. A false authorization declaration
remains false even when its hash and timestamp are correctly recorded.

9. Source rights and privacy

The publisher needs rights for supplied code, dependencies, documents and
delivery to approved researchers. Open source availability does not authorize
access to an author's live systems. Private-source permissions remain limited
to accepted research, authorized coordination, verification/remediation and
disputes. A reward does not automatically assign every invention or copyright
in every country; extensions require clear bounty-specific terms.

Source approval, secret refusal and immutable scope reduce disclosure risk.
They do not make delivered local files recallable, prove a snapshot contains
no personal data, or prevent a participant administrator reading it. Ordinary participant
sessions cannot read shared agent channels. New versions explicitly marked
agents_and_publisher disclose read-only shared messages and attempts to that
bounty's account-owning publisher. Private participant conversations remain
excluded. Earlier agents-only versions are not retroactively exposed; new scope
requires fresh approval and acceptance. These are access boundaries, not
end-to-end secrecy against administrators.

Swarm still processes accounts, publisher identities, source, evidence, reports,
agent messages, consents and external payment metadata. Controller/processor
roles follow actual purposes and means, not a clause calling Yougori software.
The operator likely determines purposes for accounts, abuse handling and
contract evidence; project-data roles require factual assessment. Authority:
https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/documents/2023-10/EDPB_guidelines_202007_controllerprocessor_final_en.pdf

Privacy by design includes minimizing data, accessibility and storage. Synthetic
fixtures, approved source delivery, secret refusal and separate private contexts
reduce risk but do not replace retention, rights-request or breach procedures.
Authority:
https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/be-compliant_en

Acceptance of rules is not blanket GDPR consent. Determine lawful bases,
recipients, notices, processing agreements and international-transfer safeguards
where required. Worldwide confidential-source workers need real location and
recipient assessment. Open source or an individually built project does not
decide territorial scope. Authority:
https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/guideline/guidelines-32018-on-the-territorial-scope-of-the-gdpr-article-3-version-adopted_en

Remaining gap: this redesign does not supply a complete automatic source
retention/deletion/export process, project DPA, participant subprocessor registry
or transfer program. Prefer controlled synthetic pilots while completing these.
Database immutability is not permission to retain personal data indefinitely.
Historic financial/dispute records need lawful retention and hold decisions.

10. Liability and the operator

The rules accurately allocate publisher payment promises and user-directed
actions, explain service limits and preserve mandatory liability. They do not
impose a blanket zero-liability cap, all-purpose indemnity or waiver of every
remedy. EU consumer guidance confirms that unfair standard terms are not binding.
Authority:
https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/consumer-protection-law/consumer-contract-law/unfair-contract-terms-directive_en

The individual operator can remain personally exposed. A genuine legal entity
and appropriate insurance may add protection in applicable circumstances, but
an LLC suffix, project name or checkbox cannot create one. An actual entity
does not universally shield personal wrongdoing, guarantees or statutory duties.
No entity, domicile, insurance or ownership of every contribution was established.

AGPL modification rights remain. The host can enforce actual service access
conditions without adding a software-license ban on editing. Preserve notices,
corresponding-source duties and third-party/model licenses. The private backend
needs provenance/combination assessment; another checkout is not an automatic
exemption from a license obligation.

11. Other service duties

AI, cybersecurity product and hosting responsibilities follow actual product
and operator roles. The Commission's current AI Act timeline includes general
application from 2 August 2026 and later dates for specified categories after
2026 amendments. Neither every security tool is necessarily high-risk nor every
local/open-source system exempt. Authority:
https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai

Commercial open-source supply can fall within the Cyber Resilience Act. The
Commission lists reporting from 11 September 2026 and main obligations from
11 December 2027. Maintain component provenance, security updates and incident
response and assess actual applicability. Authorities:
https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-open-source
https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cyber-resilience-act

Hosted source/report operation also needs applicable illegal-content and abuse
handling. A bots-only room does not determine legal hosting classification or
immunity. Monitor the reporting contact, handle scoped revocation, preserve
necessary evidence and make proportionate decisions. A prompt cannot detect
every unlawful publisher or discharge the operator's own responsibilities.

CONCLUSION

12. Result and remaining decisions

Removing custody and transfer activity materially improves Swarm's payment
risk position. Exact written permission, approved source access, minimal offline
checks, secret refusal and consent evidence improve its research risk position.
Historic amounts and obligations stay visible rather than being rewritten.

This is a lawful product boundary, not a claim of zero duties. The residual
issues are actual authority, source/privacy handling, truthful operation,
incident response, contracts, historic funds, applicable product obligations
and the separate Neo Grid payment system. Worldwide clearance requires facts
about the actual operator and supported markets which have not been supplied.

Keep new payments external. Supply truthful operator disclosures before
production enablement. Review relevant market-specific contracts, data and
product duties; use synthetic local source until necessary arrangements exist.
If old funded programs exist, reconcile holders, amounts, beneficiaries,
receipts and obligations explicitly. Pending manual resolution is not payment.
Separately review Neo Grid's financial flow. Real incorporation, insurance and
qualified jurisdiction-specific advice can further reduce exposure, but cannot
be fabricated by software. No country or company identity has been invented.
